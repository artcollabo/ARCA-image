MULLI FILM

MULLI FILM is a Seoul-based production company that makes commercials and brand films with live-action shooting and AI.

About

MULLI FILM is a commercial and corporate film production company in Seoul, run by Artcollabo Inc. Since 2016 we have produced TV commercials, brand films, and corporate and public-sector films for clients including Hyundai Motor, SK Telecom, POSCO, the Korea Tourism Organization, and the Ministry of Environment. We combine live-action shooting with generative AI. Films are directed by SANTA AHN.

What we make

  • Commercials and brand filmsTV commercials, online ads, and brand films.
  • Corporate and public-sector filmsPublic-sector campaigns, tourism films, anniversary films, technology explainers, and films for overseas trade shows.
  • Live action and AIScenes that are hard to shoot are created with generative AI and composited with live-action footage.
