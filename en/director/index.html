DIRECTOR

Director SANTA AHN

SANTA AHN is the working name of the director who leads MULLI FILM, a commercial and corporate film production company in Seoul.

Career

Studied film at the School of Film, TV and Multimedia, Korea National University of Arts (K-Arts).

Directed TV commercials for Expedia, Gmarket, and Classting, films for Hyundai Motor Group's overseas dealerships, and a CES film for Selvas AI.

Directed the documentary "Hawaii, Ukulele", screened at the Jecheon International Music & Film Festival.

Has made commercials and films under the name SANTA AHN for 15 years.
